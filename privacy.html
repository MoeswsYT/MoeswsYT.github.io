Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026. This policy explains how Pawn Launcher handles information when you visit this website or download the installer.

Information we collect

Pawn Launcher is designed to be a simple informational and download website. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information through the website. If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to include in your message, such as your name, email address, and support details.

Cookies and similar technologies

This website may use cookies or similar technologies to remember preferences, understand general site usage, and support website functionality. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some site features.

Advertising and Google AdSense

If advertising is enabled on this website, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Users may also visit www.aboutads.info to opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising.

Analytics and service providers

We may use privacy-conscious analytics or hosting providers to understand traffic and keep the website available. These providers may process technical information such as browser type, approximate location, device information, referral pages, and timestamps. We use this information in aggregate to maintain and improve the website.

Software downloads

Downloading and installing Pawn Launcher is your choice. We recommend obtaining the installer from the official website, keeping Windows and security software up to date, and scanning downloaded files before opening them. The separate Terms of Service page explains software-use responsibilities and limitations.

Children's privacy

This website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website, advertising services, or legal requirements change. The updated date at the top of this page will indicate when revisions were made.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact us through the Contact page.
